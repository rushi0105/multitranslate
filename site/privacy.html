Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

The short version

You can use MultiTranslate in two ways. Online (this website): the files you upload are stored on our server only while they are being translated and are deleted automatically after 12 hours; there are no accounts. On your own laptop: nothing is sent to us at all. In both cases the text lines being translated are sent to a third-party translation service. This website has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

1. Who we are

This website and the MultiTranslate software are operated by [BUSINESS OR PERSON NAME], [Street, City, State, PIN], India. Contact: [EMAIL].

2. Using the online version

  • Uploaded files and results are stored on our server (hosted by [HOSTING PROVIDER]) in a folder that only your browser can reach through a random job link. They are deleted automatically 12 hours after the job was created, or earlier when you press Delete. We do not open, read or share them.
  • Text lines from your files are sent to the third-party translation service the tool uses (currently public endpoints of Google Translate and MyMemory / Translated S.r.l.), which process them under their own privacy policies. Do not upload confidential or sensitive personal data if that is not acceptable to you.
  • A translation cache on the server keeps translated lines (text and translation, no file names, no identity) so repeated lines are not sent again. It is not linked to you.
  • Job history is kept in your own browser's local storage (a list of job ids). Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Server logs (IP address, time, requested URL, browser type) may be kept by the hosting provider for security and to keep the service running, typically for a few days.
  • We do not set cookies, do not run analytics or advertising scripts, and do not embed social-media widgets. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the font files are requested (Google's privacy policy).

3. Using the software on your own computer

  • Your files stay on your computer and are never sent to us.
  • Text lines being translated are sent to the same third-party translation services as above.
  • A local cache of translated lines and a local job history are stored on your computer; delete them with python translate.py --clear-cache or by deleting the folders.
  • The software contains no telemetry, crash reporting or update checks.

4. Your rights

We do not build profiles of users. Uploaded files are deleted automatically within 12 hours; you can delete a job earlier from the app. If you believe we hold data about you (for example from an email you sent us), write to [EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days. Users in India may exercise their rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; users in the EU/UK under the GDPR.

5. Children

This website and software are not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
