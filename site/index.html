Made for large sheets

Comment exports, product catalogues, subtitle files — whole files, not one sentence at a time.

Understands Hinglish"aap bahut acche ho" is converted to Hindi script first, then translated — so Punjabi and English come out complete.
Nothing breaksNumbers, emails, links, Excel formulas, {placeholders}, <tags> and #hashtags are copied through unchanged.
GlossaryFixed spellings for names and brand terms ("Mann → मान / ਮਾਨ") from one CSV file.
Resume after a crashEvery translated line is saved locally. Power cut? Run it again — it continues where it stopped.
FastBatched, multi-threaded requests; duplicate lines are translated once. In our test, 500 comments into 3 languages took about 45 seconds.
Free engines with fallbackUses public translation endpoints (Google, MyMemory). If one is unavailable the next one is used.
Job historyEvery job is listed with zip download, stop and resume. Online, your jobs are visible only in your browser and are deleted after 12 hours.
Your choice where it runsOnline: files are uploaded to our server, translated, and deleted after 12 hours. On your laptop: files never leave your computer. Text lines go to the translation engine either way. See the privacy policy.

Run it on your own laptop

For big batches (thousands of rows, whole folders) or private files: Windows, Python 3.12, one installer. Get the project folder.

App window (recommended)

Double-click  MultiTranslate  on the Desktop
# or  D:\MultiTranslate\MultiTranslate.bat

Opens in its own window. Phones on the same Wi-Fi can connect to it too.

Command line (batches, automation)

python translate.py comments.xlsx --to hi,pa,en --mode combined
python translate.py D:\data --to hi,pa               # whole folder
python translate.py sheet.xlsx --to hi --glossary names.csv
python translate.py big.csv --to hi --workers 8      # Ctrl+C, re-run = resume

Phone app

Download MultiTranslate.apk

Android 7 and up. Open the file and allow the install (it is not on the Play Store). The app connects to this website — nothing else to set up. Pick files from the phone; results download to the phone.

Own laptop running MultiTranslate? Tap Change address inside the app and enter your laptop's address.

iPhone / any browser: open the web app in Safari or Chrome and choose "Add to Home Screen".
